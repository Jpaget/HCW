2026 HAMMER CIRCLE REUNION

The Hammer Circle is pleased to confirm that the 2026 Hammer Circle Reunion will take place on 17th and 18th October at Bedford, With the track now officially booked, preparations can begin in earnest. As always, the Reunion will be a celebration of the past season’s achievements and an opportunity to bring together athletes, coaches, officials, and supporters for a weekend of competitions, coaching, and camaraderie. Further details about the event schedule, featured guests, competitions, and presentations will be announced in the coming weeks. We look forward to another memorable gathering that honours the tradition of the Circle and supports the future of hammer throwing in the UK. Mark your calendars and stay tuned for updates!
