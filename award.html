The Partizan Trophy

This solid bronze statuette of a Yugoslav Partizan was presented to the British Amateur Athletic Board by the Partizan Club of Belgrade during a British tour of the Balkans in 1951. It was presented by the BAAB to the Hammer Circle in June 1953 to be awarded annually to the most improved senior hammer thrower in Great Britain.

Despite its size of 27” (68 cm) tall and weighing 3 stone (19 kg), the trophy went missing for decades. In 2009, the ‘Sedykh Programme’ was introduced to recognise the most improved 7.26 kg performance over 60 m. In 2018, the trophy was rediscovered and displayed at the 66th Reunion in Hull.

In 2018, replicas were created as laser crystals to formally acknowledge the historic 80 m milestones of Nick Miller (7.26 kg) and Jake Norris (6 kg). The replicas are now presented as the Hammer Circle 80 m awards, while the original trophy is passed to recipients who never had the opportunity to take it home. In 2018, Paul Dickenson finally received the original trophy—44 years after first winning it.
